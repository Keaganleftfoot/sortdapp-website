3. How the platform works

  • Posters describe a task and set a budget.
  • Taskers browse available tasks and submit offers.
  • Posters review offers, shortlist, and assign a Tasker.
  • Once assigned, Poster and Tasker communicate via in-app chat to confirm scope, timing, and logistics.
  • Payment is made directly between Poster and Tasker (cash or EFT) after the work is completed to the Poster's satisfaction.
